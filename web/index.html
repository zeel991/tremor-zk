      What the proof establishes

      • The email was signed by the DKIM key the vault lists for that merchant's domain.
      • The signed body contains the merchant's total line, with exactly this amount.
      • It was sent on this date, to the inbox registered with this commitment.
      • Each email can be claimed once per vault (salted nullifier), and the payout goes only to the registered wallet.

      What it does not

      • That the purchase was business use, that you paid personally, or that it wasn't refunded. The policy agent and normal audit cover those.
      • The key list is curated by each vault owner, timelocked 24 h and checkable against DNS. The MVP merchants don't use DNSSEC, so this can't be fully trustless today.
      • Non-USD receipts pay at the company's own timelocked policy FX rate.
      • A merchant could guess which customer made a claim from an unusual amount and date.
      • Testnet: zkVerify mainnet hasn't enacted runtime 2.1.0 yet. The older ZK flavours on mainnet are marked unsound by zkVerify's own source, so we don't use them.
